# vcftools-ng v0.12.2 — Workload-Adaptive Indexing and Exact Recode Scaling

v0.12.2 replaces format-wide automatic indexing with a measured,
workload-adaptive policy while preserving VCFtools 0.1.17 exact-mode bytes.

## Highlights

- Streams full-file BCF filter/recode workloads even when CSI exists.
- Streams one-thread BGZF recode, then reuses or builds TBI/CSI from two
  threads upward.
- Streams Plain VCF at 1–2 threads and enables aligned range parallelism at
  3+ threads.
- Keeps indexed acceleration for selective BGZF/BCF region queries.
- Never overwrites an existing `.tbi` or `.csi`.
- Removes `--no-auto-index`; `--input-backend` remains the advanced override.

## Full-data exactness and performance

The 11,230,392-record, 23-chromosome, 412-sample seven-filter workload ran in
four representative input scenarios at 1/2/4/8/16/32 threads, five times per
configuration. All 120 outputs passed complete-file `cmp` and SHA validation.

VCFtools 0.1.17 was not rerun. Its v0.12.1 timings and goldens were reused only
after input, index, and oracle hashes passed.

Mean wall-clock seconds:

| Input path | Original | 1 thread | 2 threads | 4 threads | 8 threads | 16 threads | 32 threads |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BGZF VCF + TBI | 2267.88 | 309.28 | 204.91 | 112.35 | 73.93 | 52.05 | 42.32 |
| BGZF VCF + automatic CSI | 2267.88 | 308.21 | 281.03 | 167.50 | 128.60 | 107.16 | 99.59 |
| Plain VCF | 2092.91 | 285.45 | 285.22 | 89.31 | 63.71 | 47.84 | 50.61 |
| BCF adaptive stream | 1943.47 | 325.66 | 164.14 | 110.57 | 57.26 | 41.30 | 40.20 |

Speedup over VCFtools 0.1.17:

| Input path | 1 thread | 2 threads | 4 threads | 8 threads | 16 threads | 32 threads |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BGZF VCF + TBI | 7.33× | 11.07× | 20.19× | 30.68× | 43.57× | 53.59× |
| BGZF VCF + automatic CSI | 7.36× | 8.07× | 13.54× | 17.63× | 21.16× | 22.77× |
| Plain VCF | 7.33× | 7.34× | 23.44× | 32.85× | 43.75× | 41.35× |
| BCF adaptive stream | 5.97× | 11.84× | 17.58× | 33.94× | 47.05× | 48.35× |

Automatic-CSI rows include fresh index construction in every repeat. Full CPU,
RSS, hashes, exact commands, and per-run results are in the
[v0.12.2 technical record](https://github.com/VensinMa/vcftools-ng/blob/master/docs/versions/v0.12.2.md).

## Install

```bash
curl -LO https://github.com/VensinMa/vcftools-ng/releases/download/v0.12.2/vcftools-ng-v0.12.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
curl -LO https://github.com/VensinMa/vcftools-ng/releases/download/v0.12.2/vcftools-ng-v0.12.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz.sha256
sha256sum -c vcftools-ng-v0.12.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz.sha256
tar -xzf vcftools-ng-v0.12.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
./vcftools-ng-v0.12.2-linux-x86_64/bin/vcftools-ng --version
```

The portable archive targets Linux x86_64 with glibc 2.17 or newer and keeps
vcftools-ng, bcftools, HTSlib, and required non-glibc runtime libraries
together. Archive SHA-256:
`e7e04243dcbf46c4da68c33ddf5f9872cd7cc2285868a58ee1dd11592fdbee7f`.
