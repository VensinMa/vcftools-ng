# vcftools-ng v0.14.2 — Portable I/O Recovery

v0.14.2 removes the release-build regressions found by a same-host,
same-output comparison with v0.13.0 and v0.14.1. Scientific behavior and the
v0.14.1 capability planner remain unchanged.

## Highlights

- The Linux x86_64 archive now builds HTSlib 1.24 with private libdeflate
  1.25. This restores fast BGZF decompression without adding anything except
  `vcftools-ng` to the public `bin` directory.
- Full-file BCF streams receive a format-aware strict budget: one reader,
  approximately one quarter for HTSlib decode, and the remainder for compute.
  The allocation grows with explicit thread counts and has no local 32-thread
  ceiling.
- Plain VCF uses zero-copy mmap only for cacheable inputs up to 8 GiB. Larger
  files use bounded aligned `pread` workers, avoiding input-sized RSS and
  parallel page-fault/readahead regressions.
- The automatic 128-thread ceiling still applies only when `--threads` is
  omitted. Explicit values continue to intersect the effective scheduler,
  affinity, and cgroup allocation.

## Unified complete-data A/B

The release used 11,230,392 records, 412 samples, the production seven-filter
recode workload, Plain VCF output, one SSD, and one strictly serial run per
row. Real v0.13.0, v0.14.1, and v0.14.2 portable archives were tested at
`1/2/4/8/12/16/24/28/32` threads. All **81/81** initial outputs and every
post-fix Plain output passed full SHA-256 and retained-site gates. Differences
within 5% are treated as tied.

Final v0.14.2 application wall seconds:

| Input | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 24 | 28 | 32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BGZF + TBI | 214.43 | 151.02 | 77.44 | 40.72 | 34.21 | 31.68 | 26.07 | 28.84 | 28.25 |
| Plain VCF | 214.12 | 97.63 | 53.83 | 32.51 | 27.42 | 32.05 | 29.65 | 32.72 | 31.72 |
| BCF adaptive stream | 551.39 | 470.46 | 237.79 | 98.22 | 67.59 | 52.30 | 45.45 | 44.27 | 50.43 |

Relative to v0.14.1, BGZF improved by up to 1.67x, Plain by up to 1.35x, and
BCF by up to 1.99x. Most high-thread v0.13.0 rows were tied or slower than the
candidate; its BCF 1/2-thread rows exceeded the requested budget and are not
fair strict-budget baselines. At this 32-CPU host, 24/28 threads are the more
representative BCF storage knee. Program and post-`sync -f` durable time are
published separately rather than attributing a 57-59 GB writeback fluctuation
to parsing.

Full evidence is in
[`benchmarks/results/full-unified-v0142-ab`](../../benchmarks/results/full-unified-v0142-ab/README.md).

## Install

```bash
curl -LO https://github.com/VensinMa/vcftools-ng/releases/download/v0.14.2/vcftools-ng-v0.14.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
curl -LO https://github.com/VensinMa/vcftools-ng/releases/download/v0.14.2/vcftools-ng-v0.14.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz.sha256
sha256sum -c vcftools-ng-v0.14.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz.sha256
tar -xzf vcftools-ng-v0.14.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
./vcftools-ng-v0.14.2-linux-x86_64/bin/vcftools-ng --version
```

Archive SHA-256:
`95dd2c3cc431759d77bb2f7c15b9a74a7c081632895f9e80ad7f71eeb052ae41`.

The archive targets Linux x86_64 with glibc 2.17 or newer. `bin` contains only
the launcher; private bcftools 1.24, HTSlib 1.24, libdeflate 1.25, and other
non-glibc runtime libraries remain under `libexec` and `lib`.
