# vcftools-ng v0.1.0

## Scope

First high-performance vertical slice: decode VCF/BCF once and generate five
statistics in the same ordered scan.

## Parameters added

- Inputs: `--vcf`, `--gzvcf`, `--bcf`, `--input`
- Outputs: `--freq`, `--counts`, `--missing-site`, `--site-depth`,
  `--site-mean-depth`
- Execution: `--out`, `--threads`, `--batch-size`, `--compat exact`

## Validation

- Fixture: 2,300,000 real records, 23 chromosomes, 412 samples.
- Outputs: five complete statistics files.
- Oracle: five separate VCFtools 0.1.17 scans.
- Result: all files byte-identical; 1-thread and multithread ordering also
  matched.
- Goldens: `subset-{freq,counts,missing-site,site-depth,site-mean-depth}*`.

## Benchmark

| Input | Threads | Wall | CPU | Peak RSS | Exact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| BCF | 8 | 7.55 s | 398% | 64.3 MiB | PASS |
| BCF | 16 | 6.56 s | 417% | 64.6 MiB | PASS |
| BGZF VCF | 8 | 56.29 s | 140% | 64.8 MiB | PASS |
| BGZF VCF | 16 | 56.23 s | 138% | 65.1 MiB | PASS |

Original five scans totaled 352.61 seconds. BCF speedup was 46.70× at 8
threads and 53.75× at 16 threads.
