# vcftools-ng v0.4.0

## Scope

Added sample inclusion and exclusion. Every statistic, filter, and recode
operation uses only the final selected sample set.

## Parameters added

- `--keep`, `--remove`
- `--indv`, `--remove-indv`

## Validation

- Fixture: complete 2,300,000-record subset and all 412 original samples.
- Compatibility workload retained 17 samples and 63,776 sites.
- One new-engine scan produced frequency, counts, site missingness, depth,
  mean depth, and INFO-all recode.
- All six files were byte-identical to six original outputs.

## Benchmark

| Workload | Original aggregate | 8 threads | 16 threads | Exact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sample selection + six outputs | 623.49 s | 4.08 s | 3.73 s | PASS |

The original value is the sum of six required scans. Speedup: 152.82× and
167.16×.

`benchmarks/run-sample-subset.sh`
